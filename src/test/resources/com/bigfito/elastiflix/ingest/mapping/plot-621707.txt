The Vigil is a 2020 American thriller film directed by Kiran Deol. The movie revolves around a retired police officer who seeks revenge against a corrupt cop responsible for his partner's death.

At its core, The Vigil explores the themes of grief, trauma, and the blurred lines between justice and vigilantism. The protagonist's quest for vengeance is driven by his emotional pain and sense of betrayal, which serves as a catalyst for his downward spiral into darkness. As he navigates the complex web of corruption and deceit, the film raises questions about the morality of taking the law into one's own hands.

The Vigil also delves into the psychological effects of trauma on individuals and how it can shape their perceptions of reality. The protagonist's experiences have left him with a deep-seated sense of anger and resentment, which he struggles to reconcile with his own moral code. This inner conflict serves as a powerful commentary on the human capacity for both good and evil.

Furthermore, the film critiques the systemic corruption that pervades law enforcement agencies, highlighting the ways in which power and privilege can be used to silence victims and protect perpetrators. The Vigil is a thought-provoking exploration of the darker aspects of human nature, inviting viewers to consider the complexities of justice and the consequences of unchecked ambition.

Overall, The Vigil is a gripping thriller that masterfully weaves together elements of crime drama, psychological suspense, and social commentary, resulting in a cinematic experience that is both unsettling and thought-provoking.
